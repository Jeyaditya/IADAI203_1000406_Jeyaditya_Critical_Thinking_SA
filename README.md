## 1. Empathize & Define (Research & Behavioral Grounding)
Check out the Assets folder for images.
### Core Problem Statement
High school and university students experiencing academic burnout frequently use late-night scrolling as an emotional coping mechanism. Traditional screen-time blockers rely on rigid lockouts and punitive streak resets that trigger frustration and are routinely bypassed. **MindPatch** bridges emotional awareness with adaptive AI nudging to deliver low-friction, guilt-free offline resets.

### Primary & Secondary Research Insights
* **Peer Survey Findings (`n = 12` students):** Over 75% reported unplanned late-night scrolling (`10:00 PM – 1:00 AM`) driven by academic fatigue, and over 80% stated they routinely tap *"Ignore Limit"* on built-in phone timers.
* **Behavioral Science Framework:** Built on **BJ Fogg’s Behavior Model ($B = MAP$)** and **Amber Case’s Calm Technology Principles**—maximizing *Ability* via 1-tap 5–15 minute micro-resets while minimizing cognitive load.
## 2. Ideate & Sketch (SCAMPER, User Journey & Wireframes)
Using the **SCAMPER framework**, traditional screen-time blockers were reimagined—substituting harsh lockouts with mood-matched breaks, adapting streaks with monthly **Rest Day Shields**, and reversing engagement goals so sessions finish in under 90 seconds.

## 3. High-Fidelity Prototype & Compulsory Features
Built with a **Calm UI** design system (`#F8F9FA` Soft Off-White, `#8B7EC8` Muted Lavender, `#7FB069` Sage Green, `#1E293B` Deep Slate) incorporating all five compulsory features plus simulated push notifications:
1. **Mood Logging:** Interactive 5-point emotional slider with quick academic stress tags.
2. **Screen-Time Input Dashboard:** Simplified 3-category breakdown (Social, Academic, Entertainment) with live manual telemetry simulation.
3. **AI-Generated Detox Planner:** Dynamically tailors offline break durations and activities based on mood, screen load, and time of day—complete with an **Algorithmic Explainability (`Why AI suggested this`)** modal.
4. **Gratitude Journal:** Low-friction reflection space with live entry logging to shift focus from screen guilt to daily wins.
5. **Habit Tracker with Compassionate Rewards:** Forgiving streak counter featuring **Rest Day Shields** and milestone badges.

## 4. AI Logic Simulation & 5-Day Adaptive Learning
MindPatch combines deterministic safety thresholds with a **5-day adaptive receptivity model** that adjusts notification timing based on user acceptance vs. dismissal history:
* **Rule 1 (`M <= 2 AND ST > 2.5h`):** Triggers *High-Stress Intervention* (15–20 min analog grounding + breathing).
* **Rule 2 (`M == 3 AND T >= 21:00`):** Triggers *Late-Night Wind-Down Nudge* (gratitude brain-dump + screen dimming).
* **Adaptive Weighting:** After observing repeated afternoon dismissals (`Receptivity: 0.50 → 0.15`) and consistent 9:30 PM completions (`Receptivity: 0.50 → 0.92`), the scheduler automatically shifts proactive detox prompts to `9:30 PM`.

## 5. Usability Testing, Iterations & Ethical AI Reflection
### Peer Usability Testing & Before/After Iterations (`n = 3` testers)
* **Iteration 1 (Cognitive Load Reduction):** Replaced granular 6-app usage charts with 3 color-coded categories (*Social, Academic, Entertainment*), cutting screen comprehension time by ~40%.
* **Iteration 2 (Streak Compassion):** Added **Rest Day Shields** after testers noted that breaking a streak during exam week triggered anxiety.

### Ethical, Accessible & Sustainable Design
* **Privacy-by-Design:** Sensitive mood logs and gratitude entries are processed locally on-device with zero third-party ad monetization.
* **Anti-Addiction Ethics:** Sessions are engineered to conclude in under 90 seconds, preventing wellness-app screen dependency.
* **Algorithmic Transparency:** Every AI recommendation exposes its exact decision criteria via the *"Why AI suggested this"* modal.
